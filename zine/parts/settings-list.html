Settings listSettings list

Rows of settings mixing toggles, links to sub-pages and a destructive action.

Why it works: Toggles apply instantly; rows with a chevron open another page. Keep the destructive action last and in its own colour.

Try it:
Receipt: axe clean · contrast 5.73:1 · 4 tab stops · RTL ✓ · long text ✓
  • PASS axe, WCAG 2.1 A and AA: no violations in light or dark (22 rules passed).
  • PASS Tightest text contrast 5.73:1, where 4.5:1 is needed (light).
  • PASS Still passes contrast with tokens fitted to a yellow brand colour.
  • PASS Right-to-left with Arabic sample text fits a 390px phone.
  • PASS Every string 40% longer still fits a 390px phone.
  • PASS Only short hover and focus transitions (under 300ms).
