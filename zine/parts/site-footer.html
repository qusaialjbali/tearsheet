Site footerSite footer

A footer with the brand line, grouped links, a language picker and the legal row.

Why it works: Group links under short headings people scan for (Product, Company, Help). Keep the legal links and the copyright on their own quiet row.

Try it:
Receipt: axe clean · contrast 6.7:1 · 13 tab stops · RTL ✓ · long text ✓
  • PASS axe, WCAG 2.1 A and AA: no violations in light or dark (18 rules passed).
  • PASS Tightest text contrast 6.7:1, where 4.5:1 is needed (light).
  • PASS Still passes contrast with tokens fitted to a yellow brand colour.
  • PASS Right-to-left with Arabic sample text fits a 390px phone.
  • PASS Every string 40% longer still fits a 390px phone.
  • PASS No motion.
