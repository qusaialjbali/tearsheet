Pricing cardPricing card

Plan card with price, feature list and a single call to action.

Why it works: Say what happens after the click (“Start free trial”) and answer the top worry right below it (“No card required”).

Try it:
Receipt: axe clean · contrast 5.35:1 · 1 tab stop · RTL ✓ · long text ✓
  • PASS axe, WCAG 2.1 A and AA: no violations in light or dark (9 rules passed).
  • PASS Tightest text contrast 5.35:1, where 4.5:1 is needed (dark).
  • PASS Still passes contrast with tokens fitted to a yellow brand colour.
  • PASS Right-to-left with Arabic sample text fits a 390px phone.
  • PASS Every string 40% longer still fits a 390px phone.
  • PASS No motion.

Tab order

  1. link "Start free trial":

What assistive tech is given

- paragraph: Pro
- text: Most popular
- paragraph: $12 per user / month
- paragraph: For small teams that ship fast.
- list:
  - listitem: Unlimited projects
  - listitem: Version history for 1 year
  - listitem: Custom domains
  - listitem: Priority email support
- link "Start free trial":
  - /url: "#"
- paragraph: 14 days free · No card required

Automated checks in CI (axe in Chromium, reduced motion on). They catch a lot, not everything: still try it with a screen reader.

See it in the zine npx -y tearsheet add pricing-card

Needs the shared tokens.css once per project.
